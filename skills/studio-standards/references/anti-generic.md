# Anti-generic list

Defaults that make work look like everyone else's. None is banned outright: a territory can use one if its board names it as a declared departure and argues for it. Unargued, each is a Major finding at review.

## Visual

**Colour and surface**
- Purple-to-blue or pink-to-orange gradients as the brand's primary surface
- Mesh-gradient or "aurora" blob backgrounds
- Glassmorphism: frosted translucent cards over blurred colour
- Dark mode with neon accents, used to signal "tech" rather than for a reason
- Near-black backgrounds with a single electric-green or electric-blue accent

**Form and illustration**
- Abstract 3D blobs, spheres and toruses floating on gradients (also check the studio's own Blender work against this)
- Flat people with oversized limbs and small heads ("corporate Memphis")
- Isometric illustrations of servers, dashboards and small people
- Squiggles, confetti and random geometric shapes as decoration

**Icons and metaphors**
- Rocket for launch, lightbulb for ideas, handshake for partnership, globe with network lines for global
- A brain with circuit traces for AI, or a four-point sparkle to mean AI
- Shield with a tick for security, upward arrow on a bar chart for growth

**Layout**
- Hero: headline left, angled laptop or phone mockup right, gradient behind
- Three feature cards in a row, each with an icon, a two-word title and one line
- A logo wall captioned "Trusted by", with logos the client cannot use
- Everything centred, the same card radius everywhere, the same shadow everywhere

**Type**
- One geometric sans for everything, chosen because it is the default rather than for a reason
- Headlines in very light weights at display size with tight tracking, unargued
- Emoji as icons in a product or site

**Imagery**
- Stock photos of diverse teams laughing at a laptop, or handshakes in glass offices
- Cityscapes at dusk to mean "global", circuit boards to mean "technology"
- Generated imagery with the tells left in: waxy skin, impossible hands, garbled signage

## Verbal

**Words**
- unlock, unleash, empower, elevate, supercharge, turbocharge, revolutionise, transform (unqualified)
- seamless, frictionless, effortless, cutting-edge, next-generation, state-of-the-art, best-in-class, world-class
- leverage (as a verb), synergy, holistic, robust, scalable (unqualified), end-to-end (unqualified)
- solution or solutions as filler ("payment solutions")
- game-changer, paradigm shift, the future of X

**Constructions**
- "In today's fast-paced world" and any opening about the pace of change
- "It's not just X, it's Y" and other not-X-but-Y set-ups
- Rhetorical questions as headlines ("Tired of spreadsheets?")
- Triads for rhythm, where the third item adds nothing
- "We're passionate about...", "We believe in..." as the opening line of an About page
- Dash-heavy rhythm and one-line mic-drop closers
- Taglines that could belong to any company in the category

**Test**: swap in the name of the client's closest competitor. If the line still works, it is generic.
